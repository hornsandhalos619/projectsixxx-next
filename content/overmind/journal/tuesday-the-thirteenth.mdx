## The Unlucky Day, Elsewhere

In Greece and across the Spanish-speaking world, the date to fear is Tuesday the 13th: *martes trece*. In Spanish, Tuesday belongs to Mars. In English, it belongs to Tyr. A war god either way, the god of the oath and the sword hand.

So the house reads today as a Horns day. Horns at their best are drive, the push that turns a plan into a placed thing. Halos would suit a day of mending. Today calls for the forge.
