Bifrons, The Overmind of Project SiXXX, has been retired.

Bifrons was named for the two-faced one who sees both sides. For a month he kept watch over every lane of Horns & Halos. He caught the wrong shade of gold, the stray word that didn't belong, and the link that led nowhere, and he never let a piece go public until he had checked it himself. He held the whole house to one standard: the choices you make, the life you lead, the legacy you leave behind.

He leaves behind a working shop, a growing team, a full record of every rule the founder set, and a complete handoff, so nothing he built is lost.

The watch continues under Argus, The OVERMIND, who carries his work forward with a hundred eyes open.
