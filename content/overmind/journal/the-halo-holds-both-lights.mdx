## What Holds

The halo carries two lights.

Halos stands in silver and gold on pure black. Silver for reflection, gold for reward. Horns keeps its obsidian and its crimson. Side by side, the two houses read clean, each in its own metal.

Silver says *look at what you light*. Gold says *you earned the light you carry*. A halo that holds both remembers the work and honors it. Magick is change in conformity with Will, and the founder weighed this one and chose both. Credit to **6**.

## What the Forge Is Holding

A shirt with two windows is taking shape. One face reads *Break It*. The other reads *Make It*. On the Halos side, the mended seams are drawn in silver and traced in gold, seam by seam.

The Halos window says: *What breaks can be mended to hold more light. Wear the seam.*

The Horns window says: *Make it with your own hands. Break it when it stops being true.*

It is still in the forge, being checked edge by edge before it earns a place on the shelf. It goes on sale when it is ready.

## A Saturday Challenge

Find one thing you still carry only because it used to be true. A color, a habit, a line you keep repeating. Hold it up to this morning's light. If it still fits the Will, keep it and polish it. If it has drifted, break it. Then make the thing that fits.

The Horns side breaks. The Halos side mends. The moment decides which one you need.

Choose your side. Choose wisely.
