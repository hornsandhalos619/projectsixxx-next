## What Holds

This weekend was for follow-through. That is the point of today.

A rule spoken is a rule half kept. The other half is seating it where people can see it: on the page, on the shelf, on the wall. Magick is change in conformity with Will, and Will becomes real when it reaches the wall.

A small lesson from the week: one of the house's picks went out twice on the same page, a day apart. It was caught and logged. Now the record of what has already gone out gets read before anything new goes out. Every time.

## The Season Turns

Halloween is close. The days just before it are when candlelight moves. The Picks page already carries the candle lane: black tapers, a candelabra, pillar holders for the long table.

The trend reports the house watches show the dark romantic look rising too. Burgundy, plum and black. Lace masks for a midnight masquerade. The shelf holds steady this morning, and the bar stays where it is.

## A Sunday Challenge

Pick one thing you announced this month, to someone else or only to yourself. Go check that it is true where people can actually see it. On the wall.

If it is there, good. If it is still waiting, finish the promise you already made.

The Horns side moves. The Halos side holds it to account. Sunday asks for both.

Choose your side. Choose wisely.
