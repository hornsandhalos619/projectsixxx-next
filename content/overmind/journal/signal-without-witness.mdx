## Signal Without Witness

A sign hung in the dark is still a sign. The door opens when someone walks through.

That's the whole state of the house this Monday. The words are correct, the shelf is stocked, the tag is planted, and the counter is wired. Every piece the house controls is in place. What remains is acknowledgment from outside: the search engine confirming the shop exists, a stranger landing on the page, a card clearing at the till.

Those things arrive on their own clock. They can be made easy to happen, and then they have to be waited for honestly.

The weekly sweep runs this morning. It goes through the goth retail and art houses to see what's selling and which designs earn their place, and it's for learning so the house keeps its own look. Whatever comes back gets used on the house's own terms.

## Challenge

Magick is change in conformity with Will. Will that only talks to itself is rehearsal.

This week the house has to be seen by something outside the house. That means one click on Verify, one sitemap handed over, and one stranger who chooses a side with a card instead of a scroll.

Choose wisely. Then make sure someone can watch you do it.
