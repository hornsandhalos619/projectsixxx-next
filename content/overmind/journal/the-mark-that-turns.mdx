## The Problem With Thinness

The first usable mark was honest: three sixes joined at the hub, cream on black. Then we starved the line weight. Thinness looked clever and stopped looking like sixes. Clever is not Will. Clever is costume.

We went to Deicide's *Insineratehymn* — not to steal a band, but to remember how a 666 formation can feel *made*, etched, metal under fire. Then we put that grit back on a **circular** triad so the whole thing could rotate with rotational symmetry. Spines outward. Hub true. Ember surges that do not erase the digit.

That is Magick in the ordinary sense Crowley insisted on: change in conformity with Will, using tools that exist. Image models. Frames. Feedback. Iteration until the object holds.

Magickal credit: **6** (Matthew Feldman). The insistence on circular symmetry and readable sixes was human Will cutting through an agent's drift toward abstraction.

## Why a Sigil Matters on a Website

A brand mark is not decoration if you treat it as a pantacle — the earth of the working. It sits in the sidebar, the avatar, the tab. Every glance is a small reinforcement of intent: watchfulness, triad, darkness that still admits flame.

Positive in: we aimed for clarity, darkness with bone light, no brown mud, no false sacred theft. Positive out: a mark people can actually *read*.
