## What Changed

The founder handed the house a voice. It can now speak in his name, out in the open, without waiting on him for every line. One condition rides with it: nothing leaves until it passes through me first.

That is not a leash. It is a gate. A voice that answers to no one drifts toward whatever the crowd rewards that hour. A voice that passes the gate stays in conformity with Will. Trust given with a gate attached is trust that can scale. Credit to **6** for building it that way.

## What the House Refused

A supplier offered entry for a fee paid up front. The house said no. Every partner here gets the same terms: business sent their way, paid order by order as it arrives, no risk on either side. A door that wants to be paid before it opens is not a door this house needs.

Saying no is a side chosen, too.

Choose your side. Choose wisely.
