Black pillar candles, tall black tapers and a five-arm black candelabra. Everything an altar needs for the long nights is together in one place. [See the altar picks](/picks#altar)

*Interview with the Vampire* (Halos) is lush, grieving and elegant. *Frankenstein* (Horns) is the original creation myth of the dark, and it stays wise and humane every time you reread it. [See the gothic library](/picks#library)

## Wear the season
A black heart choker for every day, and a stainless signet ring in black or silver so you can choose your side. [See steel and black](/picks#steel-and-black)
