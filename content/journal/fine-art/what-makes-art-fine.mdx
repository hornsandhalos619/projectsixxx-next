## The Word We Overuse

Fine is a blade people keep in the drawer and then wave at dinner. It is supposed to mean the work can stand without a costume, without a committee, without a caption that does the lifting. We use it when we mean expensive. We use it when we mean old. We use it when we mean we did not want to say *good* and sound common.

The house uses it as a cut. Fine is the work after the mood has been asked to leave.

## Craft Before Consensus

Craft is the part you cannot vote into existence. Edge, weight, interval, the way a line dies. Consensus is a room. Rooms are useful. Craft is the work.

If the room loves it and the object is still lazy, the object is still lazy. If the room is silent and the object holds, the object holds. We do not confuse applause with a joint that was cut square.

## Commerce Is Weather

Money moves. That is its job. A sale can fund the next night. A sale cannot baptize a weak piece. An unsold piece is not automatically honest, and a sold piece is not automatically compromised. Commerce is weather. Craft is the building.

On this shelf we will point at tools, books, and instruments because people ask. The pointer is a door. If you buy through a link, the house may receive a fraction. The work does not get finer because of that fraction.

## The Quiet Tests

Does it still work when you take the biography off it.

Does it survive a second look in worse light.

Did the maker make a decision you can feel with the volume down.

Would you keep it if the name on it went blank.

Would you keep it if the price went blank.

These are the questions we actually use. Fail one and you stay. Fail all of them and you are costume.

## Outside the Cut

Sacred-symbol theft. Living-artist cosplay. Horror-movie fonts. A pentagram used like a logo. A cross used like jewelry for a brand. Purple fog that thinks it is depth. A story about pain that needs the audience to finish the sentence.

Costume darkness is loud. Fine darkness is specific. Est. in Darkness is a date.
